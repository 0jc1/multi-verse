# Apeverse Worlds Reborn - v1.1 change log (2026-11-23)

This pack is a complete revised copy of the Worlds Reborn master brief, not a replacement project.

## Character realism - NEW section 34A
- Human NPC faces and bodies should be near-photoreal in conversation range, while alien/ape visual identities remain fantastic.
- High-fidelity rigged close-range hero models with realistic eyes, skin, hair, facial animations, clothing, hands, movement.
- Progressive LOD, material/texture optimizations and practical real laptop FPS acceptance; no pretense that mass photoreal NPCs are free.
- Mutt, Cough, Nuni, Steve, Dino, Chiang Mai/Da Nang crowd NPCs first. No unauthorized exact real-person likeness.

## Songkran - NEW section 35A
- Much busier and wilder Chiang Mai water festival with crowd layers, temporary tubs/mini-pools, refill points, drenching and music.
- Held bucket: one-click large water throw, refill.
- Held water gun: hold-click continuous jet, release stops, refill.
- Held water bottle: click to throw forward splash with a punch-like thrust (NOT a punch).
- On mobile same semantics on Splash/Use; empty hands preserve punch and all other held-item controls remain stable.
- Reactions, water particles, sound, screen droplets and optional reduced effects.
- Test transitions and actual real-world play; do not rely on test assertions alone.

## Preservation
All world maps, city plans, history-era concepts, original sound/music rules, basketball/DJ/minimap work, backups, original world IDs and other requirements from the earlier pack remain intact.
