# M17: The ApePhone (`js/apephone.js`)

One device for your life in the Apeverse, built on the game's existing menus.

**Opening it.** **Z** opens the phone on desktop; on touch screens there's a **📱** button at the top right. It shows:
- a status bar with the time, weekday, credits and energy
- an app grid with unread badges

**Z used to open the People panel.** The people list now lives in the phone as **Contacts**.

## Apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| 💬 Messages | Texts generated by the world (below). Read a thread and reply with 👍, "On my way!" or "Thanks!". Replying to a resident adds +1 friendship, once a day. |
| 👥 Contacts | The M11 people panel: who you know, what they're doing now, locate. |
| 🗺️ Maps | Every place in the current world with its distance; **Route** sets the GPS line on the minimap. Also opens the travel map. |
| 📅 Calendar | Today's weekday, then upcoming events by date: your gig, DJ night, tapas invitations, festival nights, and flat and shop lease ends. |
| 🎸 Gigs | The M10 gig guide and band panel. |
| 🏦 Bank | Balance and the last 25 transactions (the game's own transaction log). |
| 📓 Journal | Tasks (the existing quests) and milestones across the 2.0 systems: residents met, friends, band, songs, gigs, DJ nights, festival headliners, reef log, shop sales, table orders. Unreached milestones are hidden as "…". |
| 🎵 Music | Now playing, next track, and whether your local library is set up on this machine. |
| 🚗 Garage · 🏠 Home · 🏪 My shop | The M7, M8 and M9 panels. |
| 🐠 Reef log | The Koh Tao species log. |
| 🎒 Backpack · ⚙️ Settings | The existing backpack and settings. |

**Back navigation.** Any app opened from the phone shows **◀ 📱 Phone** to return. The same panels opened elsewhere don't show it.

## Messages from the world

Each message is generated at most once:

| From | When | Says |
|---|---|---|
| Friends (M11 residents, friendship 30 or more) | every other day | an authored line in their own voice, e.g. Carmen's torrijas, Kofi busking at Sol, Yuki's light on Gran Vía |
| The resident who invited you | 18:00 on the day | "See you at 21:00 at Bar La Esquina!" |
| Sala Malasaña | 19:00 on gig day | a reminder |
| The DJ venue | 19:00 on DJ-night day | a reminder |
| 🎪 Festivals | 12:00 on festival days | Hanging Bazaar, Sairee Fire Night |
| 🏪 Your shop | each morning | yesterday's number of sales and revenue |
| 🏠 Landlord | the day your lease ends | a warning |

New messages show a toast and a badge on the 📱 touch button.

**Save.** A new optional field, `S.phone = { msgs, keys }`, holding at most 60 messages.

## Verification

`tools/tests/suite_phone.js`, real GPU (laptop on battery), desktop **43/43** and phone mode (`-Mobile`, 915 × 412 landscape) **43/43**. In landscape the home screen switches to 7 columns, so all 14 apps fit without scrolling.

- **Home screen:** Z opens it with 14 apps, a status bar and the touch button. It fits the screen width.
- **Back navigation:** Contacts opened from the phone has ◀ Phone, which goes back to the home screen. The same panel opened elsewhere has no back button.
- **Madrid texts:** Carmen texts as a friend; the invitation reminder arrives; the gig reminder arrives at 19:00. Each message arrives only once, and the badge counts unread messages.
- **Shop and landlord:** "yesterday 2 sales, 115 credits"; "lease ends tonight".
- **Messages app:** the inbox lists texts; opening a thread marks it read (unread count −1); a reply is saved and adds +1 friendship.
- **Calendar:** a new gig, a festival and the shop lease, sorted by date.
- **Bank:** the balance and the shop rent payment.
- **Maps:** at least 5 places; Route sets the minimap GPS and closes the phone.
- **Journal:** milestones with ✅ and ⬜, counting friends.
- **Other apps:** the reef log lists 7; Music opens; Backpack and Settings open.
- **Festival text:** Saturday brings the Hanging Bazaar text.
- **Persistence:** messages and replies are saved; the real save is untouched.

**Regression (real GPU):** npc 84/84, band 62/62, home 61/61, biz 46/46, drive 39/39, consume 47/47, madrid 79/79, integration 131/131.

**Consumables test fix.** One run measured the photosensitivity guard at 28°/s: the test sampled at a different moment in the frame than the guard applied. The guard now reports its own timestamp, and the test measures with it: 19.1°/s in two runs. The guard code itself is unchanged.

**Test fix.** One calendar check failed because the test had advanced the day, so the booked gig was correctly marked as missed. The test now books a new one first.
